Weave, design 02

A redesign of the mini bar, the tray and the full app. The chrome carries no colour; each list's own colour is the only hue on screen, and it marks that list's tasks. Clickable prototypes, light and dark.

01 · Mini bar and sheet

The bar says what is left and the one thing to know: an overdue task or the next time. Type "tomorrow 6pm" and the sheet shows the due time before you press Enter.

02 · Hide to tray

One action hides the bar into the macOS menu bar, the Windows tray or an Android notification. The same sheet opens from there, with Show bar and Pause reminders.

03 · Weave app

Opens on Up next: everything due today across lists, on a day thread from 07:00 to 23:00. Lists, task details, archive and trash, and settings, on desktop and phone.

Direction

Dealt by the taste skill's app draw and adopted whole. Rationale and open questions are in README.md beside this page.

Style
Achromatic ledger (seed 686191426): ink chrome, one near-black primary, one hue on the data
Hue
The list's own colour, on its checks and its marks on the thread
Type
Geist, 400 and 500; 13 / 14 / 16 / 24 px; tabular figures on every time and count
Shape
Radius 8 cards, 6 controls; 1px borders; shadows only on what floats (bar, sheet, menus)
Only Weave has
The day thread: today's tasks as marks on one line, the part already lived drawn in ink
