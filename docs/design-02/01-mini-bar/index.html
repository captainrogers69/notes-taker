01 · Mini bar

  1. Click the faded bar. Type call the bank tomorrow 10am and watch the line under the field, then press Enter.
  2. Tick the overdue bill, then close the sheet: the bar now shows the next time instead of "1 overdue". While the sheet is open the bar hides its count, since the sheet says it.
  3. Click the list name (or Backspace in an empty field) to switch or create a list.
  4. Drag the bar anywhere, or focus it and use the arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps), or press Move to corner in the sheet. Near the bottom, the sheet opens upward.
